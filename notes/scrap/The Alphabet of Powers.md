Chapter 2: Atomic Operations
Mastering the basic building blocks of word mathematics.
2.1 The Fundamental Operations
Just as atoms combine to form molecules, letters combine to form words in our exponential system. Let's master the basic operations:
Addition in Exponents = Multiplication of Letters
When we multiply two letters, we add their exponents:

Rule: 10ᵃ × 10ᵇ = 10^(a+b)
Example: b × d = 10² × 10⁴ = 10⁶ = f

Subtraction in Exponents = Division of Letters
When we divide two letters, we subtract their exponents:

Rule: 10ᵃ ÷ 10ᵇ = 10^(a-b)
Example: h ÷ c = 10⁸ ÷ 10³ = 10⁵ = e

"aba" Analysis:

For any palindrome "xyx": result = x² × y
